Zero-shot prompting gives an instruction with no examples, while few-shot prompting includes input-output examples so the model can imitate the pattern and format. A good prompt states the role, task, context, constraints and required output format. Chain-of-thought prompting asks the model to reason step by step, which often helps arithmetic and logic. Hallucination is fluent but unsupported or false output, and grounding the model in provided documents and asking it to quote sources reduces it. Temperature controls randomness: low temperature is more deterministic and high temperature is more varied.
