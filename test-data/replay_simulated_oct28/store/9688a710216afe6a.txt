Preemptive scheduling can take the CPU from a running process, while non-preemptive scheduling lets it run until it blocks or finishes. First-Come First-Served is simple but suffers the convoy effect. Shortest Job First gives the minimum average waiting time but needs burst prediction and can starve long processes. Round Robin gives each process a fixed time quantum; a very large quantum behaves like FCFS and a very small one is dominated by context-switch overhead.
