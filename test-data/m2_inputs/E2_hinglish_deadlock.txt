Deadlock (Operating Systems, Hinglish notes)
Deadlock tab hota hai jab do ya zyada processes ek dusre ke resources ka wait karte rehte hain aur koi bhi aage nahi badh paata.
Deadlock ke liye chaar conditions ek saath true honi chahiye: mutual exclusion, hold and wait, no preemption aur circular wait.
Mutual exclusion ka matlab hai ki ek resource ek time par sirf ek process use kar sakta hai.
Hold and wait mein process ek resource pakad ke rakhta hai aur doosre resource ka wait karta hai.
Circular wait mein processes ek chain banate hain, jahan har process agle process ke resource ka wait karta hai.
Deadlock prevention ka tareeka hai in chaar conditions mein se kam se kam ek ko todna, jaise saare resources ek saath maangna.
Resource allocation graph mein cycle ho to deadlock ka khatra hota hai.
